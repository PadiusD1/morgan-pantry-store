# FRC registration, selected board data, and receipts

## Scope

Owner-requested repair, based on main `d6c2f532596f8b57b0d48bacaeb11738c41d2ddf`.
No production data migrations, historical attribution rewrites, new subscriptions, or dependency changes.

## Changes

- Donors default to newest additions, retain alphabetical sorting and expanded contact search, and explicitly refresh on page mount/focus. Staff can request a refresh. A successful save clears an obsolete filter and identifies the confirmed record. Donors with no recorded donations remain visible.
- Confirmed donor/partner registrations publish both directory and donation-source caches before revalidation, protecting against a stale in-flight read or a failed follow-up read. Receiving retains the canonical source for subsequent entries rather than repeatedly creating new partners.
- Donor and client registration forms offer Partner; receiving offers inline Donor/Partner; checkout offers a partner registration dialog without discarding the cart. Partner records remain clients of type `partner`, not donor or student duplicates. Explicitly selected partner identifiers are not subjected to student-ID format rules, and cannot be changed through checkout.
- The board selector controls service metrics, receiving/current stock, and monthly/item breakdowns. Preview, CSV and selected print output share a data model. Unselected data is absent from the exported document, not merely hidden. Full/default exports keep their existing format. Empty selection refuses export. Full item ledgers are exported even when the on-screen preview is limited.
- Receipt rows retain the quantities, weights and estimated unit values returned in saved transaction lines. Request fulfilment responses include saved transaction items too. Totals show recorded weight, estimated item value, an equal estimated pantry-savings deduction, and $0 due for free distributions. Missing measurements are explicit; values are estimates, not cash payments or tax valuations. Print windows remain usable and blocked popups produce a visible error.
